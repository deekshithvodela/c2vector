About C2Vector Studio

The definitive high-precision raster-to-vector engine built purely in native browser Web Workers. Convert raster images, logos, and graphics to scalable SVG vectors and master print-ready PDF suites locally with zero cloud telemetry.

Why C2Vector Was Built

Most vectorizers on the web today suffer from one of three fatal flaws: they force you behind a predatory paywall, upload your private intellectual property and photos to opaque cloud servers, or wrap an uncalibrated generative AI model that invents phantom lines and distorts geometric precision.

c2vector was created to restore mathematical determinism to digital vectorization. By executing peer-reviewed computer vision algorithms (Canny edge detection, Otsu thresholding, CIELAB color quantization, and Potrace continuous Bézier fitting) directly inside client browser Web Workers, c2vector delivers infinite scalability, zero data leakage, and exact reproducibility.

Conversion Deep Dives & Supported Formats

Whether you are preparing corporate branding for vinyl cutting, converting camera photographs into silkscreen art plates, or rasterizing illustrations for responsive web apps, c2vector offers specialized pipelines tailored to your input media.

Universal Image to Vector Engine

Seamlessly convert raster images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF) with transparent backgrounds into clean W3C SVG 1.1 vectors. Transparent pixels are preserved or knocked out cleanly, anti-aliased edge halos are eliminated, and spot colors are mapped directly to discrete vector path groupings.

High-Resolution Photo to Scalable Vector

Transform compressed photographs into infinitely scalable vector art. Includes an automated pre-filtering stage with bicubic Hermite resampling and bilateral spatial/photometric Gaussian smoothing to purge compression artifacts before curve tracing.

Logo, Typography & Graphic Engine

Engineered specifically for corporate logos, typography, monograms, and line icons with adaptive natural spot color discovery (up to 32 colors with zero truncation). Employs tight straight-line tolerance (optTolerance: 0.08) and corner preservation (alphaMax: 0.15) to guarantee razor-sharp 90-degree corners and retain small punctuation marks like dots on 'i'.

Multi-Tonal Silk Screen Art Plates

Decompose any photograph into 4-tone and 8-tone positive/negative color plates, 1-bit Otsu monochrome silhouettes, and laser-cut stencil bridges. Ideal for serigraphy screen printing, CNC router carving, and vinyl signage.

How to Convert Images to Scalable Vectors

1 Select Engine Mode

Choose Photo / Decal Mode for complex multi-color photography and detailed portraits, or Logo & Typography Mode for corporate branding, line art, and geometric graphics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an image to SVG without losing quality?
c2vector converts raster images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF) into vector SVG files by tracing exact pixel contours using continuous cubic Bézier splines and corner-preserving Potrace algorithms. Transparent backgrounds are knocked out automatically, and spot colors are preserved with zero quality degradation at any resolution.
Does c2vector use generative AI or machine learning models?
No. c2vector guarantees 0% Generative AI. We do not use AI at all to convert your files. Every vector curve, color plate, and edge is calculated using 100% deterministic mathematical algorithms—sub-pixel Canny edge gradients, Otsu variance minimization, CIELAB color space quantization, and Schneider-Ramer-Peucker spline fitting executing purely in your local browser memory.
Are my uploaded photos or company logos private and secure?
Yes, 100%. All processing runs locally inside your browser's Web Workers. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server, cloud storage bucket, or third-party API. When you close or refresh the tab, all in-memory buffers are immediately reclaimed.
How does the dedicated Logo & Typography engine preserve 90-degree corners and multi-color palettes?
The dedicated Logo Engine bypasses bilateral blurring to preserve raw pixel edges, groups anti-aliased transition pixels into true dominant spot colors with adaptive natural discovery (supporting up to 32 spot colors without artificial truncation), and configures the curve fitting engine with strict straight-line tolerance (optTolerance: 0.08) and corner preservation (alphaMax: 0.15) to maintain sharp 90-degree angles and tiny punctuation marks.
Can I use the exported SVG vectors for commercial CNC, vinyl cutting, and silk screen printing?
Yes. c2vector generates clean, standard W3C SVG 1.1 files with explicit viewBox coordinates, closed paths, and separate tonal plates specifically structured for laser cutting, vinyl sign plotting, silk screen stencil printing, embroidery, and CAD fabrication.
Why is deterministic mathematical vectorization superior to AI vectorizers?
Generative AI vectorizers invent unpredictable visual artifacts, warp straight geometry into wavy lines, and add phantom details. Deterministic mathematical vectorization produces exact, reproducible, and mathematically provable geometric boundaries that respect the original image.

Privacy Policy & Security Guarantee

100% In-Browser Execution

c2vector is engineered as an offline-capable, client-side progressive web application. Every calculation runs entirely inside your device's browser memory via Web Workers.

Zero Cloud Storage & Zero File Uploads

Your uploaded photographs, generated SVG plates, and compiled PDFs never travel over the network to any server or cloud service.

Zero Tracking, Cookies & Third-Party Ad Pixels

c2vector does not set advertising cookies, persist tracking identifiers, or sell analytics data. Usage metrics are gathered using GoatCounter—a privacy-first, GDPR-compliant open-source analytics service that does not record personal identifiable information (PII).

Disclosure & Technical Specifications

Deterministic Mathematical Vectorization (0% AI)

c2vector is not a generative AI tool, machine learning model, or diffusion wrapper. All outputs are derived deterministically using peer-reviewed computer vision and geometric curve-fitting algorithms.

Vector PDF & SVG Compatibility

All exported SVG files adhere strictly to the W3C SVG 1.1 standard. Exported vector PDFs are compiled using jsPDF and svg2pdf.js entirely in client memory without external rendering binaries.
